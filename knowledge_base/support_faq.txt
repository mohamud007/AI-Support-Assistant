
COMPANY CUSTOMER SUPPORT FAQ

1. Return Policy
Customers can request a return within 7 days of delivery.
The product must be unused and in its original packaging.
Refunds are processed within 5 to 7 business days after return approval.

2. Shipping Policy
Standard delivery takes 3 to 5 business days.
Express delivery takes 1 to 2 business days.
Customers receive a tracking number after the order is shipped.

3. Cancellation Policy
Customers can cancel an order before it is shipped.
Orders that have already been shipped cannot be cancelled directly.
Customers can request a return after delivery if eligible.

4. Customer Support
Customer support is available Monday to Saturday,
from 9 AM to 6 PM.
Customers can contact support at support@example.com.

5. Payment Policy
We accept credit cards, debit cards, and UPI payments.
If a payment succeeds but the order is not confirmed,
customers should contact support with their transaction details.
